Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This explains what ANCAP LIFE collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address, username, password (stored hashed by our login provider, never readable by us), and date of birth.
  • Game data: your saved game (company name, cash, buildings, progress) and the information needed for the shared world, such as your company name, net worth and the buildings you own, which other players can see.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting and login providers for security and to run the service.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and save your game (performing the service you asked for).
  • To check your age and keep children under 13 off the service.
  • To show the leaderboard and shared world, and to keep the service secure and fair.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show advertising. Your date of birth and email are never shown to other players; your username and company name may be.

Who processes it

We use Supabase (login and database) and Vercel (website hosting). They process data for us under their own privacy and security terms and may store it in data centres outside your country. The Game also loads open-source code and fonts from public CDNs (cdnjs, jsDelivr, Google Fonts), which can see your IP address when you load the page.

Children

The Game is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn we have, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us.

How long we keep it

We keep account and game data while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account we delete your profile, email and saved game, except where we must keep something to meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or California law) you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to some uses, or complain to your data protection authority. Contact us below and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

Accounts are protected by hashed passwords and row-level access rules so each player can read only their own save and profile. No online service is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above.
